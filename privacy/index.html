Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 1, 2026

Destia ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Onboarding Responses: Name, age, gender, personal goals, values, dreams, and other responses you provide during the onboarding process. This information is used solely to personalize your manifestation experience.
  • Usage Data: Gratitude entries, app interaction data, and preferences stored locally on your device.
  • Anonymous Authentication: We use Firebase Anonymous Authentication to create a unique, anonymous user ID. We do not collect your email, phone number, or any other identifying information through this process.
  • Subscription Data: Purchase and subscription information is managed by RevenueCat and Apple. We do not store your payment details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate personalized manifestation scripts and affirmations using AI
  • To create personalized audio content tailored to your goals
  • To track your gratitude entries and streaks
  • To manage your subscription status

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Anthropic (Claude API): To generate personalized manifestation content. Your onboarding data is sent to Anthropic's API to create custom scripts. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to their processing of this data.
  • OpenAI (Text-to-Speech): To convert manifestation text into audio. The text content is sent to OpenAI's TTS API. OpenAI's privacy policy applies.
  • Firebase: For anonymous authentication and cloud data storage.
  • RevenueCat: For subscription and purchase management.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your onboarding data and gratitude entries are stored locally on your device and in Firebase Firestore under your anonymous user ID. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Audio files are cached locally on your device.

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as you use the app. You can delete your data at any time by signing out of the app, which will reset your local data. To request deletion of cloud-stored data, please contact us.

6. Children's Privacy

Destia is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of personalized content generation

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the app.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@destia.app.
